StreetwearHub 3D model assets
=============================

All models in this folder are third-party assets released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain). No brand logos, names, or textures from other clothing brands are used.

Source: 3D Assets (https://3dassets.dev) - "Clothing Rail and Wardrobe" CC0 pack.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Naming convention: <SKU>.glb, e.g. SH-TSHIRT-001.glb. A product's SKU can be
mapped to a model by passing the modelUrl prop to ProductViewer3D.

All files are self-contained GLB (no external textures, no Draco decoder needed).
